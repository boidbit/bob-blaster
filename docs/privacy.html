Blob Blaster – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Blob Blaster (Android package com.boidbit.blobblaster, "the app") is a game published by Boidbit ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with information. The short version: the app collects no personal data and sends nothing off your device.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect, receive, store on any server, share or sell any personal information. The app has no accounts or sign-in, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics, no crash-reporting, no trackers and no third-party SDKs. We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, device identifiers or advertising ID.

Information stored on your device

To remember your game, the app saves the following in its private local storage on your phone: the level you reached, your coin count, your best level, and your settings (sound, vibration, screen shake, background). This stays on your device and is never sent to us or anyone else. It is deleted when you uninstall the app, clear the app's data in Android settings, or use "Reset progress" inside the game.

Permissions

PermissionWhy
VibrateGives haptic feedback when blobs pop. You can switch it off in the game's Settings.
InternetDeclared automatically by the app framework (Capacitor). The game runs fully offline and makes no network requests. Its fonts and images are packaged inside the app.

Third parties

The app does not share data with third parties because it does not collect any. Google Play, through which the app is distributed, may process information about your download and device under Google's own privacy policy, which we do not control.

Children

The app contains no ads, no purchases, no chat, no links out and no data collection, so nothing in it targets or tracks children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. All your game data is under your control on your own device (see above for how to erase it).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated "Last updated" date. If a future version of the app starts collecting any data, this policy and the Google Play Data safety section will be updated before that version is released.
