Blob Blaster – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Blob Blaster (package com.boidbit.blobblaster) is a game by Boidbit. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: it collects nothing.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no trackers and no third-party SDKs.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (level reached, coins, and settings such as sound, vibration and background) is saved only in the app's local storage on your phone. It never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or using "Reset progress" in Settings deletes it.

Permissions

The app asks for the vibration permission only, so it can give haptic feedback when you pop blobs. You can switch vibration off in Settings. (The Android internet permission is declared by the app framework, but the game works fully offline and makes no network requests.)

Children

The game contains no ads, no purchases, no chat and no data collection, so there is nothing that targets or tracks children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated date.
